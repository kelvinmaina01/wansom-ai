# LEGAL SKILL: BURUNDI JURISDICTION (REPUBLIC OF BURUNDI)

## 🏛️ Constitutional Framework
- **Primary Law**: Constitution of the Republic of Burundi (2018).
- **System**: Civil Law based (influenced by Belgian/French law).

## 📄 Key Statutes & Acts
- **Employment**: Labour Code (2020), Decree-Law on Social Security.
- **Business**: Code of Private and Public Companies (2006).
- **Property**: Land Code (Code Foncier du Burundi).
- **Digital/Tech**: Law on Electronic Communications (2013).

## ⚖️ Judicial System
- **Judiciary**: Supreme Court, Constitutional Court, Court of Appeal.
- **Specialized Courts**: Commercial Courts, Labour Courts.

## 🌍 Regional Integration
- **EAC**: Member of the East African Community.
- **COMESA**: Member and active participant in regional trade.

## ✍️ Drafting Standards
- **Currency**: Burundi Franc (BIF).
- **Languages**: Kirundi, French, and English. Most legal documents are officially in French/Kirundi.
