# LEGAL SKILL: EAST AFRICA REGIONAL (EAC & COMESA)

## 🌍 Regional Framework
- **Primary Body**: East African Community (EAC).
- **Core Treaty**: Treaty for the Establishment of the East African Community.
- **Unified Markets**: EAC Common Market Protocol (Free movement of goods, labor, services, and capital).

## ⚖️ Cross-Border Principles
- **Harmonization**: Mutual recognition of professional qualifications and academic certificates.
- **Dispute Resolution**: East African Court of Justice (EACJ) - handles interpretation of the EAC Treaty.
- **Trade**: COMESA (Common Market for Eastern and Southern Africa) rules of origin and simplified trade regimes.

## 📄 Key Regional Instruments
- **EAC Customs Union Protocol**.
- **EAC Monetary Union Protocol** (Implementation ongoing).
- **AfCFTA**: Alignment with the African Continental Free Trade Area.

## ✍️ Cross-Border Drafting Standards
- **Governing Law**: Must explicitly state the primary jurisdiction while acknowledging regional protocols.
- **Language**: English is the official language of the EAC.
- **Arbitration**: Preference for regional centers like KIAC (Kigali) or LCIA-MIAC (though outside EAC, often used).
