# LEGAL SKILL: RWANDA JURISDICTION (REPUBLIC OF RWANDA)

## 🏛️ Constitutional Framework
- **Primary Law**: Constitution of the Republic of Rwanda (2003, revised 2015).
- **System**: Transitioned from Civil Law to a Hybrid (English Common Law influence).

## 📄 Key Statutes & Acts
- **Employment**: Law No. 66/2018 regulating labour in Rwanda.
- **Contracts**: Law No. 45/2011 governing contracts.
- **Property**: Law No. 27/2021 governing land in Rwanda.
- **Business**: Law No. 007/2021 governing companies.
- **Digital/Tech**: Law No. 058/2021 relating to the protection of personal data and privacy.

## ⚖️ Judicial System
- **Judiciary**: Supreme Court, Court of Appeal, High Court.
- **Specialized Courts**: Commercial Courts and Commercial High Court.
- **Citation**: Use official Rwandan Gazette references.

## 🌍 Regional Integration
- **EAC**: Member of the East African Community.
- **Commonwealth**: Member since 2009 (Common Law influence).

## ✍️ Drafting Standards
- **Currency**: Rwandan Franc (RWF).
- **Languages**: Kinyarwanda, English, and French (All official). Document drafts should specify the prevailing language.
