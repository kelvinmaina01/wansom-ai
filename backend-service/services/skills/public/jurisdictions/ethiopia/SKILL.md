# Ethiopia Jurisdiction Rules

## Legal framework
- **Constitution**: Federal Democratic Republic of Ethiopia Constitution (1995).
- **Contract law**: Civil Code of Ethiopia (1960) (Proclamation No. 165/1960) — Still the primary law for obligations.
- **Labour**: Labour Proclamation No. 1156/2019.
- **Companies**: Commercial Code of Ethiopia (2021) — Replaces the 1960 Commercial Code for businesses.
- **Land**: State-owned; private ownership is NOT permitted. Users hold "Use Rights" only.
- **IP**: Copyright and Neighbouring Rights Protection Proclamation No. 410/2004.
- **Data**: Computer Crime Proclamation No. 958/2016; Personal Data Protection Bill (pending).
- **Court system**: Federal Supreme Court → Federal High Court → Federal First Instance Courts (Parallel State courts).
- **Dispute resolution**: Ethiopian Arbitration and Conciliation Centre (EACC); Ethiopian Center for Dispute Resolution.

## Civil Law System (French-influenced)
Ethiopia uses a **CIVIL LAW** system based on the 1960 Civil Code (drafted by French jurist René David).
- Contracts interpreted according to Civil Code provisions.
- **Good faith (bonne foi)** is a binding obligation (Article 1713).
- Common law concepts (e.g., consideration) do not directly apply.
- New Commercial Code (2021) modernizes company law significantly.

## Mandatory document requirements
- **Physical address**: Must include *Kebele* and *Woreda* (administrative units).
- **TIN**: Taxpayer Identification Number is mandatory for all legal individuals/entities.
- **Language**: Amharic is the official working language. Contracts in Amharic take priority.
  - English contracts are common but Amharic versions are recommended in court.
- **Notarization**: Required for company documents, land-related deeds, and some commercial contracts.
- **Registration**: Certain contracts must be registered with the relevant authority (e.g., MOTI, EIC).

## 💰 Taxation (Federal Income Tax Proclamation No. 979/2016)
- **Employment Income Tax**: Progressive rates from 0% to 35% (Monthly threshold: 600 ETB tax-free).
- **Business Income Tax (Corporate)**: Standard rate is **30%** for companies.
- **VAT**: Standard rate is **15%** (Registration threshold applies).
- **Turnover Tax (TOT)**: 2% for services, 10% for others (for entities below the VAT threshold).
- **Withholding Tax**: 2% on local supplies of goods/services; 5% on imports.
- **Capital Gains**: 15% for buildings, 30% for shares.
- **Stamp Duty**: Applicable on leases, contracts, and capital increases at various small percentages.

## Currency and financial references
- **Currency**: Ethiopian Birr (**ETB**).
- **Foreign exchange**: Strictly controlled by the National Bank of Ethiopia (NBE).
- USD/EUR contracts for offshore payment require NBE approval.
- **Repatriation**: Profits require documentation and NBE clearance.
- **Rate**: Refer only to the NBE official exchange rate.

## Key labour minimums (Labour Proclamation 1156/2019)
- **Annual leave**: 14 working days after 1 year, +1 day per additional year (Max 28 days).
- **Maternity leave**: 30 days prenatal + 60 days postnatal = 90 days total.
- **Sick leave**: Up to 6 months with a medical certificate (first month full pay, etc.).
- **Pension**: 7% employee contribution + 11% employer contribution.
- **Hours**: 48 hours/week standard; 60 hours/week maximum with overtime.
- **Overtime rate**: 1.25x (10 PM - 6 AM), 1.5x (Day), 2x (Public Holiday), 2.5x (Weekly Rest Day).
- **Probation**: Maximum 60 working days.

## Governing law clause (standard)
```
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with
the laws of the Federal Democratic Republic of Ethiopia, including
the Civil Code of Ethiopia (Proclamation No. 165/1960) as applicable.
Any dispute shall be referred to arbitration at the Ethiopian
Arbitration and Conciliation Centre (EACC) in Addis Ababa.
```

## Common mistakes to avoid
- **Land Ownership**: Avoid "purchase" terms; use "lease" or "transfer of use rights".
- **Forex**: Do not promise USD payments without explicit NBE clearance.
- **Language**: Avoid English-only for major local contracts; use bilingual (Amharic/English).
- **Proclamations**: Do not cite the 2003 Labour Proclamation or the 1960 Commercial Code (for companies).
- **Registration**: Forgetting to register with the Ethiopian Investment Commission (EIC) for foreign investments.
