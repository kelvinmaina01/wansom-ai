# LEGAL SKILL: TANZANIA JURISDICTION (UNITED REPUBLIC OF TANZANIA)

## 🏛️ Constitutional Framework
- **Primary Law**: Constitution of the United Republic of Tanzania (1977).
- **Structure**: Union between Tanganyika and Zanzibar; specific laws may apply to Zanzibar exclusively.

## 📄 Key Statutes & Acts
- **Employment**: Employment and Labour Relations Act (2004), Labour Institutions Act (2004).
- **Contracts**: Law of Contract Act [Cap 345 R.E. 2019].
- **Property**: Land Act No. 4 of 1999, Village Land Act No. 5 of 1999.
- **Business**: Companies Act (Cap 212), Partnership Act [Cap 344].
- **Digital/Tech**: Personal Data Protection Act (2022).

## ⚖️ Judicial System
- **Judiciary**: Court of Appeal, High Court, Resident Magistrate's Courts.
- **Specialized Courts**: High Court (Labour Division), High Court (Commercial Division).
- **Citation**: Use [Year] TZHC or [Year] TZCA.

## 🌍 Regional Integration
- **EAC**: Member of the East African Community.
- **SADC**: Member of the Southern African Development Community.

## ✍️ Drafting Standards
- **Currency**: Tanzania Shilling (TZS/Tsh).
- **Formatting**: Formal legal headings, explicit reference to R.E. (Revised Edition) for statutes, bilingual (English/Swahili) where required by client.
