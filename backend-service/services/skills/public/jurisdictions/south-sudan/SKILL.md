# LEGAL SKILL: SOUTH SUDAN JURISDICTION (REPUBLIC OF SOUTH SUDAN)

## 🏛️ Constitutional Framework
- **Primary Law**: Transitional Constitution of the Republic of South Sudan (2011).
- **System**: Developing legislative framework based on English Common Law and Customary Law.

## 📄 Key Statutes & Acts
- **Employment**: Labour Act (2017).
- **Contracts**: Contracts Act (2008). 
- **Property**: Land Act (2009).
- **Business**: Companies Act (2012), Investment Promotion Act (2009).

## ⚖️ Judicial System
- **Judiciary**: Supreme Court (Constitutional Court), Court of Appeal, High Court.
- **System**: Judiciary is currently under reform to integrate customary courts with statutory ones.

## 🌍 Regional Integration
- **EAC**: Member of the East African Community (Joined 2016).
- **IGAD**: Founding member and active participant.

## ✍️ Drafting Standards
- **Currency**: South Sudanese Pound (SSP).
- **Language**: English is the official language for government and legal business.
