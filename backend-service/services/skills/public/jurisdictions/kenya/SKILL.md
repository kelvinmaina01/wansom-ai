# LEGAL SKILL: KENYA JURISDICTION (REPUBLIC OF KENYA)

## 🏛️ Constitutional Framework
- **Primary Law**: Constitution of Kenya (2010).
- **Supremacy**: Any law inconsistent with the Constitution is void to the extent of the inconsistency (Article 2).
- **Bill of Rights**: Strictly enforced (Chapter 4).

## 📄 Key Statutes & Acts
- **Employment**: Employment Act (2007), Labour Institutions Act (2007), Labour Relations Act (2007), Work Injury Benefits Act (WIBA).
- **Contracts**: Law of Contract Act (Cap 23), Section 3(3) requirements for written/signed contracts.
- **Property**: Land Act (2012), Land Registration Act (2012), Sectional Properties Act (2020).
- **Business**: Companies Act (2015), Partnerships Act (2012), Insolvency Act (2015).
- **Digital/Tech**: Data Protection Act (2019), Computer Misuse and Cybercrimes Act (2018).

## ⚖️ Judicial System
- **Supreme Court of Kenya**: Final arbiter on constitutional matters.
- **Court of Appeal**: General appellate jurisdiction.
- **Specialized Courts**: High Court, Employment and Labour Relations Court (ELRC), Environment and Land Court (ELC).
- **Citation**: Use [Year] eKLR for reporting.

## 🌍 Regional Integration
- **EAC**: Member of the East African Community. 
- **Protocols**: EAC Common Market Protocol, Customs Union Protocol.
- **COMESA**: Member and signatory to regional trade agreements.

## ✍️ Drafting Standards
- **Currency**: Kenyan Shilling (KES/Ksh).
- **Formatting**: Numbered clauses, formal definitions section, "Executed as a Deed" where required.
