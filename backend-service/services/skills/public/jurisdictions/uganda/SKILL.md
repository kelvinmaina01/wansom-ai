# LEGAL SKILL: UGANDA JURISDICTION (REPUBLIC OF UGANDA)

## 🏛️ Constitutional Framework
- **Primary Law**: Constitution of the Republic of Uganda (1995).
- **System**: Unitary state with a hybrid of English Common Law and Customary Law.

## 📄 Key Statutes & Acts
- **Employment**: Employment Act (2006), Employment Regulations (2011), Workers Compensation Act (Cap 225).
- **Contracts**: Contracts Act (2010), replacing the old English Law of Contract Act.
- **Property**: Land Act (Cap 227), Registration of Titles Act (Cap 230).
- **Business**: Companies Act (2012), Partnership Act (2010).
- **Digital/Tech**: Data Protection and Privacy Act (2019).

## ⚖️ Judicial System
- **Judiciary**: Supreme Court, Court of Appeal (Constitutional Court), High Court.
- **Specialized Divisions**: Industrial Court (for labor disputes), Commercial Court.
- **Citation**: Use [Year] UGLR or [Year] HCB.

## 🌍 Regional Integration
- **EAC**: Member of the East African Community.
- **Treaties**: EAC Treaty, COMESA (Common Market for Eastern and Southern Africa).

## ✍️ Drafting Standards
- **Currency**: Uganda Shilling (UGX/Ush).
- **Formatting**: "In witness whereof...", clearly defined choice of law clauses, use of Advocates Act standards for execution.
