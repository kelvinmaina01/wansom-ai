# LEGAL SKILL: COURT FILINGS (CIVIL PROCEDURE)

## 🏛️ Jurisdictional Procedural Codes
- **Kenya**: Civil Procedure Act (Cap 21) & Civil Procedure Rules 2010.
- **Uganda**: Civil Procedure Act (Cap 71) & Civil Procedure Rules (SI 71-1).
- **Tanzania**: Civil Procedure Code (Cap 33).
- **Regional**: East African Court of Justice (EACJ) Rules of Procedure.

## 📄 Mandatory Filing Components
1. **The Heading (Title of the Court)**: Must precisely name the Court, Registry, and Case Number.
2. **Parties**: Full names, description (Plaintiff/Defendant), and address for service (Physical & Email per modern Electronic Filing rules).
3. **The Plaint (Statement of Claim)**:
   - Must contain a concise statement of material facts.
   - Distinct sections for: Parties, Jurisdiction, Facts of the Case, and Prayers (Relief).
   - Paragraphs must be numbered sequentially.
4. **The Verifying Affidavit**:
   - Required for most originating processes.
   - Deponent must swear that the facts stated in the Plaint are true to the best of their knowledge.

## ✍️ Drafting Standards (Style & Tone)
- **Language**: Formal Legal English (Amharic for Ethiopian local courts).
- **Citations**: All case law must be cited with the correct year and Law Report reference (e.g., [2024] eKLR).
- **Consistency**: Party names must be identical across all documents (Plaint, Summons, Notice of Motion).

## 🛠️ Common Filing Types
- **Notice of Motion**: Accompanied by a supporting affidavit.
- **Chamber Summons**: For interlocutory applications (e.g., temporary injunctions).
- **Memorandum of Appearance**: Filed by the Defendant's counsel to acknowledge service.
- **Defense & Counterclaim**: Must be filed within the statutory period (usually 14-21 days depending on jurisdiction).

## ⚠️ Critical Procedural Rules
- **Certificate of Urgency**: Must be filed if an application needs to be heard ex-parte or within 24 hours.
- **Witness Statements**: Many EAC jurisdictions (like Kenya) now require witness statements to be filed *at the same time* as the Plaint.
- **Signature & Stamp**: All documents must be signed by the Advocate on record and bear their official stamp/LSK number (if applicable).
