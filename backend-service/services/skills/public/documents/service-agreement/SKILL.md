# LEGAL SKILL: SERVICE AGREEMENT (CONSULTANCY)

## ⚖️ Legal Status
- **Independent Contractor**: Explicitly state that the service provider is NOT an employee. No entitlement to leave, pension, or health insurance.
- **Taxation**: Service provider is responsible for their own Income Tax and VAT (where applicable).

## 📄 Mandatory Clause Checklist
1. **Scope of Work (SOW)**: Detailed description of deliverables and timelines.
2. **Payment Terms**: Milestones, deposit (if any), and invoicing cycle.
3. **Intellectual Property (IP)**: "Work for Hire" clause – IP transfers to Client upon full payment.
4. **Termination for Convenience**: Ability for either party to exit with X days' notice.
5. **Indemnity**: Consultant indemnifies Client against third-party IP claims.
6. **Force Majeure**: Standard regional clause for unforeseen events.

## ✍️ Drafting Standards
- Use "The Client" and "The Service Provider" (or Consultant).
- Focus on "results" rather than "hours worked".
