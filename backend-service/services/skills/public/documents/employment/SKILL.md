# LEGAL SKILL: EMPLOYMENT CONTRACT

## 📜 Statutory Foundations
- **Kenya**: Employment Act 2007.
- **Uganda**: Employment Act 2006.
- **Tanzania**: Employment and Labour Relations Act 2004.
- **Regional**: EAC Common Market Protocol (Right of establishment and work).

## ⚖️ Mandatory Statutory Minimums (Cannot be waived)
- **Probation**: Maximum 6 months (Kenya) or 6 months (Uganda).
- **Annual Leave**: Minimum 21 working days (Kenya) or 21 days (Uganda).
- **Maternity Leave**: 3 months (Kenya) or 60 working days (Uganda).
- **Sick Leave**: 7 days full pay, 7 days half pay (Kenya) or 1 month full pay (Uganda).
- **Notice Period**: Usually 1 month or payment in lieu of notice.

## 📄 Mandatory Clause Checklist
1. **Position & Duties**: Clear job title and description.
2. **Remuneration**: Stated gross salary, allowances (House, Medical), and statutory deductions (NHIF, NSSF, Housing Levy).
3. **Working Hours**: Standard 45-52 hours per week depending on sector.
4. **Place of Work**: Specify town and possibility of relocation.
5. **Termination & Retirement**: Clear notice periods and retirement age (usually 60).
6. **Confidentiality & IP**: Assigning all IP created during employment to the employer.

## ✍️ Drafting Standards
- Ensure all statutory levies are mentioned (e.g., "subject to all statutory deductions").
- Use "The Employer" and "The Employee".
