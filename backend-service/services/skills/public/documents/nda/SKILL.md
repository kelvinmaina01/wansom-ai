# LEGAL SKILL: NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT (NDA)

## 🛡️ Core Confidentiality Standard
- **Definition of Confidential Information**: Must be broad but specific to the trade secrets and proprietary data involved.
- **Exclusions**: Information already public, independently developed, or obtained from a third party.

## ⚖️ East African Specificities
- **Duration (Survival)**: Standard practice is 2-5 years post-termination. For trade secrets (codes, formulas), it can be indefinite.
- **Non-Compete Clauses**: Must be "reasonable in scope and duration" to be enforceable in Kenya and Uganda. Unreasonable restrictions are void.
- **Choice of Law**: Must align with the Governing Law selected (e.g., Laws of Kenya).

## 📄 Mandatory Clause Checklist
1. **Purpose Clause**: Specifically state *why* the information is being shared (e.g., "to evaluate a potential partnership").
2. **Standard of Care**: Disclosee must use the same degree of care as they use for their own confidential info.
3. **Equitable Relief**: Explicitly mention that monetary damages may not be sufficient (allows for injunctions).
4. **Return or Destruction**: Requirement to return or destroy data upon request or termination.
5. **Governing Law & Dispute Resolution**: Must match the Registry triggers.

## ✍️ Drafting Tone
- Use "The Disclosing Party" and "The Receiving Party" consistently.
- Avoid legalese where a plain English term suffices (e.g., use "keep secret" alongside "confidentiality").
