# LEGAL SKILL: PARTNERSHIP AGREEMENT / JOINT VENTURE

## ⚖️ Legal Frameworks
- **Kenya**: Partnership Act 2012.
- **Uganda**: Partnership Act 2010.
- **Tanzania**: Partnership Act (Cap 190).
- **Consortiums**: Often used for large infrastructure projects in the region.

## 📄 Mandatory Clause Checklist
1. **Capital Contribution**: Specify the initial contribution of each partner (Cash, Assets, IP).
2. **Profit & Loss Split**: Defaults to equal shares unless specified otherwise.
3. **Management & Decision Making**: Define "Majority" vs "Unanimous" decisions.
4. **Admission & Withdrawal**: Rules for bringing in new partners or exiting.
5. **Dissolution**: Procedural steps for winding up the business.
6. **Non-Compete**: Prohibiting partners from starting a competing firm.

## ✍️ Drafting Standards
- Use "The Partners" or "The Joint Venturers".
- Explicitly state the "Trading Name" of the partnership.
