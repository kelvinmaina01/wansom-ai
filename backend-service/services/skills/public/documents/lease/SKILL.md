# LEGAL SKILL: LEASE / TENANCY AGREEMENT

## 📜 Statutory Foundations
- **Kenya**: Land Act 2012 & Land Registration Act 2012.
- **Uganda**: Land Act (Cap 227) & Registration of Titles Act.
- **Tanzania**: Land Act 1999 (Unit Titles Act).

## ⚖️ Mandatory Provisions
- **Security Deposit**: Usually 1-3 months of rent.
- **Rent Review**: Clause defining how and when the rent can be increased (e.g., every 2 years).
- **Quiet Enjoyment**: The landlord's covenant not to interfere.
- **Maintenance**: Clear split between Landlord (Structural) and Tenant (Internal).

## 📄 Mandatory Clause Checklist
1. **Premises Description**: Precise description of the land and LR Number.
2. **The Term**: Commencement date and duration.
3. **Rent & Service Charge**: Clear breakdown of payments.
4. **User Clause**: Restricting how the premises is used (Residential/Commercial).
5. **Assignment & Subletting**: Usually forbidden without Landlord's consent.
6. **Eviction Procedure**: Standard regional notice period (usually 30 days).

## ✍️ Drafting Standards
- Use "The Landlord" (or Lessor) and "The Tenant" (or Lessee).
- Always include a "Schedule of Fittings".
