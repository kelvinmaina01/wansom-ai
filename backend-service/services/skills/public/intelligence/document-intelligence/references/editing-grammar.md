# Editing Grammar & Clause Templates

## Drafting Principles for EA Legal Documents

1. **Use defined terms consistently** — introduce a definition once ("the Company"), use it every time.
2. **Numbered clauses** — hierarchical numbering: 1. / 1.1 / 1.1.1. Never use letters for main clauses.
3. **Present tense for obligations** — "the Buyer shall pay" not "the Buyer will pay" (shall = obligation in EA drafting).
4. **Avoid shall for conditions** — use "if" or "where", not "shall if".
5. **No "and/or"** — always choose one. If both are meant, say "either or both".
6. **Numbers in words then figures** — "fourteen (14) days" for critical periods.
7. **Currency with country prefix** — "Kenya Shillings (KES)" / "Uganda Shillings (UGX)", not bare "$".
8. **Dates in full** — "15th day of March 2024", not "15/3/24".

## Rewrite Style Guide

### PROFESSIONAL style
- Target: court filings, formal legal correspondence, arbitration submissions
- Register: formal, complete, precise
- Sentence length: medium to long, syntactically complex
- Vocabulary: full legal vocabulary, no colloquial shortcuts
- Example transformation:
  - Original: "You can end this contract anytime with 30 days notice"
  - Professional: "Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving not less than thirty (30) days' written notice to the other party, such notice to be delivered in accordance with Clause [N] hereof."

### SIMPLE style
- Target: client explanations, lay summaries, plain language addenda
- Register: plain English, conversational but accurate
- Sentence length: short, single idea per sentence
- Vocabulary: no Latin, no defined-term jargon unless explained
- Example transformation:
  - Original: "In the event of a material breach of the representations and warranties contained herein..."
  - Simple: "If either side breaks a key promise made in this contract..."

### PRO-CLIENT style
- Target: demand letters, negotiation drafts, protective amendments
- Register: firm, clear, positions the client's interests strongly
- Key moves: convert "may" → "shall", add "immediately", add consequences for non-performance,
  remove limitation of liability from client side, tighten counterparty obligations
- Example transformation:
  - Original: "The Supplier shall use reasonable endeavours to deliver within 30 days"
  - Pro-Client: "The Supplier shall deliver the Goods on or before [Date], time being of the essence. Failure to deliver by that date shall entitle the Buyer to: (a) cancel this Agreement immediately; and (b) claim a refund of all amounts paid together with interest at the rate of 14% per annum from the date of payment."

## Standard EA Clause Templates

### FORCE MAJEURE (EA version)
```
Force Majeure

[N.1] Neither party shall be in breach of this Agreement or liable for any delay in performing, 
or failure to perform, any of its obligations under this Agreement if such delay or failure 
results from a Force Majeure Event.

[N.2] In this Agreement, "Force Majeure Event" means any event beyond a party's reasonable 
control, including but not limited to: acts of God; flood, drought, earthquake, or other 
natural disasters; epidemic, pandemic, or public health emergency declared by a competent 
authority; terrorist attack, civil war, civil commotion or riots; war, armed conflict; 
nuclear, chemical, or biological contamination; collapse of buildings, fire, explosion, 
or accident; any act of government or regulatory authority including sanctions; or disruption 
to critical infrastructure including power, water, telecommunications, or transport networks.

[N.3] A party seeking to rely on this clause shall:
  (a) notify the other party in writing within [7] days of the Force Majeure Event arising; and
  (b) use all reasonable endeavours to mitigate the effect of the Force Majeure Event.

[N.4] If the Force Majeure Event continues for more than [90] days, either party may terminate 
this Agreement on [30] days' written notice without liability to the other.
```

### DISPUTE RESOLUTION — TIERED (EA preferred)
```
Dispute Resolution

[N.1] Negotiation. If any dispute arises out of or in connection with this Agreement, 
the parties shall first attempt to resolve it by negotiation between senior representatives 
of each party. Either party may initiate this process by written notice to the other. 
The parties shall meet within [14] days of such notice.

[N.2] Mediation. If the dispute is not resolved within [30] days of the notice referred to 
in clause [N.1], either party may refer the dispute to mediation administered by the 
[Nairobi Centre for International Arbitration / Centre for Arbitration and Dispute Resolution 
(CADER) Uganda / Tanzania Arbitration Centre — choose as appropriate].

[N.3] Arbitration. If the dispute is not resolved by mediation within [60] days of the 
commencement of mediation, the dispute shall be finally resolved by arbitration in 
accordance with the [Arbitration Act (Cap. 49), Laws of Kenya / Arbitration and Conciliation 
Act (Cap. 4) Uganda — choose as appropriate]. The arbitration shall be:
  (a) conducted by a sole arbitrator agreed between the parties, or appointed by the 
      [NCIA/CADER] if not agreed within [14] days;
  (b) seated in [Nairobi/Kampala/Dar es Salaam];
  (c) conducted in the English language; and
  (d) subject to the [NCIA/CADER] Rules in force at the date of commencement.

[N.4] Nothing in this clause prevents either party from seeking urgent interim relief 
from a court of competent jurisdiction.
```

### DATA PROTECTION (Kenya DPA 2019 aligned)
```
Data Protection

[N.1] Each party shall comply with all applicable data protection legislation including, 
where applicable, the Data Protection Act, 2019 (Kenya) and any regulations made thereunder 
(together, "Data Protection Laws").

[N.2] Where one party processes personal data on behalf of the other in connection with 
this Agreement:
  (a) it shall act only on the documented instructions of the data controller;
  (b) it shall implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to ensure a 
      level of security appropriate to the risk;
  (c) it shall not engage another processor without the prior written consent of the 
      data controller; and
  (d) it shall, at the data controller's election, delete or return all personal data 
      on termination of this Agreement.

[N.3] The processor shall notify the controller without undue delay (and in any event 
within 72 hours) upon becoming aware of a personal data breach.

[N.4] Each party warrants that it is registered with the Office of the Data Protection 
Commissioner as required by the Data Protection Act, 2019.
```

### CONFIDENTIALITY / NDA
```
Confidentiality

[N.1] Each party (as "Recipient") undertakes to keep confidential all Confidential Information 
received from the other party (as "Discloser") and to use it solely for the purposes of 
this Agreement.

[N.2] "Confidential Information" means all information disclosed by the Discloser to the 
Recipient in connection with this Agreement that is designated as confidential, or that 
reasonably should be understood to be confidential given the nature of the information 
and the circumstances of disclosure. It excludes information that:
  (a) is or becomes publicly available other than through the Recipient's breach;
  (b) was already known to the Recipient at the time of disclosure; or
  (c) is independently developed by the Recipient without use of Confidential Information.

[N.3] The Recipient shall:
  (a) limit disclosure to its employees, officers, advisers, and sub-contractors on a 
      need-to-know basis, and ensure each is bound by obligations of confidence no less 
      strict than this clause; and
  (b) notify the Discloser immediately upon becoming aware of any actual or threatened 
      breach of this clause.

[N.4] This clause shall survive termination of this Agreement for a period of [3] years.
```

### LIMITATION OF LIABILITY (balanced EA version)
```
Limitation of Liability

[N.1] Nothing in this Agreement limits or excludes either party's liability for:
  (a) death or personal injury caused by negligence;
  (b) fraud or fraudulent misrepresentation; or
  (c) any other liability that cannot be limited or excluded by applicable law.

[N.2] Subject to clause [N.1], neither party shall be liable to the other, whether in 
contract, tort (including negligence), breach of statutory duty, or otherwise, for:
  (a) loss of profits;
  (b) loss of business;
  (c) loss of anticipated savings;
  (d) loss of goodwill; or
  (e) any indirect or consequential loss,
arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.

[N.3] Subject to clauses [N.1] and [N.2], each party's total aggregate liability to 
the other arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall not exceed 
[the total fees paid or payable in the 12 months preceding the event giving rise to the claim / 
KES [X] — choose as appropriate].
```

### TERMINATION (dual — for cause and for convenience)
```
Termination

[N.1] Termination for Convenience. Either party may terminate this Agreement at any time 
by giving not less than [30/60/90] days' written notice to the other.

[N.2] Termination for Cause. Either party may terminate this Agreement with immediate 
effect by written notice if the other party:
  (a) commits a material breach of this Agreement and (where such breach is capable of 
      remedy) fails to remedy it within [14] days of written notice requiring it to do so;
  (b) becomes insolvent, makes an arrangement with creditors, goes into liquidation 
      (other than for the purpose of a solvent reconstruction), or has a receiver or 
      administrator appointed over its assets; or
  (c) undergoes a change of control without the prior written consent of the terminating party.

[N.3] On termination for any reason:
  (a) all outstanding payments become immediately due;
  (b) each party shall promptly return or destroy the other's Confidential Information; and
  (c) clauses [list surviving clauses] shall survive termination.

[N.4] Termination shall be without prejudice to any rights or remedies that have accrued 
prior to termination.
```

### IP OWNERSHIP
```
Intellectual Property

[N.1] All intellectual property rights in and to the [deliverables / works / software / 
documents] produced by [Party] under or in connection with this Agreement ("Works") shall, 
upon creation, vest in and be the absolute property of [Client / Commissioning Party].

[N.2] [Service Provider] hereby assigns to [Client], with full title guarantee, all present 
and future intellectual property rights in the Works throughout the world for the full 
period of protection.

[N.3] [Service Provider] shall execute all documents and do all acts and things reasonably 
required to vest the rights referred to in this clause in [Client] and to enable [Client] 
to apply for, obtain, and maintain protection for such rights.

[N.4] [Service Provider] retains all intellectual property rights in its pre-existing tools, 
methodologies, and know-how. Where the Works incorporate pre-existing materials, 
[Service Provider] grants [Client] a royalty-free, non-exclusive, perpetual licence 
to use such pre-existing materials as incorporated in the Works.
```

### ENTIRE AGREEMENT / MERGER CLAUSE
```
Entire Agreement

[N.1] This Agreement, together with the Schedules and any documents expressly incorporated 
by reference, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties relating to its subject 
matter and supersedes all prior agreements, representations, warranties, negotiations, 
and understandings, whether written or oral, relating to such subject matter.

[N.2] Each party acknowledges that in entering into this Agreement it has not relied on, 
and shall have no right or remedy in respect of, any statement, representation, assurance, 
or warranty (whether made innocently or negligently) that is not set out in this Agreement.

[N.3] Nothing in this clause shall limit any liability for fraud.
```
