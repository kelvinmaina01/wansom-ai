# LEGAL SKILL: DRAFTING DESIGN SYSTEM
- **Structure**: Use `<div>` wrapping.
- **Headers**: `<h1>` for main title, `<h2>` for section headings, `<h3>` for sub-sections.
- **Paragraphs**: `<p>` for body text.
- **Emphasis**: `<b>` or `<strong>` for cross-references or parties.
- **Lists**: `<ul>` or `<ol>` for clause items.
- **Alignment**: Use `text-align: justify` for a formal legal appearance.
- **Prohibited**: No `<html>`, `<head>`, or `<body>` tags. 
- **Style**: Use professional, clear, and unambiguous legal English.
