# LEGAL SKILL: NDA (NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT)
- **Standard Clauses**: Definition of Confidential Information, Exclusions, Obligations, Term, Return of Materials.
- **Jurisdictions**: Needs specific Choice of Law and Jurisdiction clauses for EAC countries.
- **Confidentiality Periods**: Standard 2–5 years after termination.
- **Indemnity**: Specific indemnity for breach of confidentiality.
- **Non-Circumvention**: Include where business development/partnerships are involved.
- **Governing Law**: Must explicitly name the EAC country (Kenya, Uganda, etc.).
